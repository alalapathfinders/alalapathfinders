Meet the Founder

The Al-Ala Pathfinders was founded by a passionate student who recognized the need for a youth-driven platform rooted in Islamic values. With a vision to empower young Muslims to become leaders in their communities, the founder embarked on a journey to create a space where faith, character, and leadership could flourish. Through dedication and hard work, the founder has built a vibrant community that continues to inspire and guide others on the Straight Path.
